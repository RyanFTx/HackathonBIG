- Game Logic Documentation - Slither.io Clone with Language Learning
-
- This game is a clone of Slither.io, but instead of orbs, the player encounters words with translations from another language (currently Chinese).
-
- Gameplay:
- - The player controls a snake-like avatar that moves around the game area.
- - Scattered throughout the map are "word orbs", each displaying a word and its translation.
- - The player's objective is to eat the correct translations:
- - If the player eats an orb with the correct translation, their avatar grows in size.
- - If the player eats an orb with an incorrect translation, their avatar shrinks and they lose one of their three lives.
- - The game continues until the player loses all lives or achieves a high score by consistently eating correct translations.
-
- Educational Purpose:
- - The game helps players learn and reinforce vocabulary in a foreign language (Chinese).
- - By associating words with their correct translations, players improve their language skills in an engaging and interactive way.
-
- Key Features:
- - Snake movement and growth mechanics inspired by Slither.io.
- - Word orbs with randomized correct and incorrect translations.
- - Life system: player starts with three lives and loses one for each incorrect translation eaten.
- - Visual and gameplay feedback for correct and incorrect choices.
